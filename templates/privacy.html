Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense records you log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal financial data with third-party services. If this ever changes — for example, to add optional integrations — we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided in your account settings.
